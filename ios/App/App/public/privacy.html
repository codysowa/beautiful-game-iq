Beautiful Game IQ Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

Beautiful Game IQ is a coaching application for organizing youth soccer teams, planning lineups, tracking games, and reviewing team information. This policy describes the information the app collects and how it is used.

Information you provide

When you create an account or use the app, we may collect your email address, display name, team information, player roster information, game information, lineup information, and other information you choose to enter into the app.

How we use information

We use this information to authenticate your account, provide the team-management and coaching features you request, maintain your team data, respond to support requests, and diagnose reported bugs.

Bug reports

If you submit a bug report, we may store the report details together with your account, team, screen, app version, platform, page URL, and technical user-agent information so we can investigate the problem.

Service providers

Beautiful Game IQ uses third-party services to provide parts of the application, including Supabase for authentication and application data, RevenueCat for subscription management, and Google AdMob for advertising in the free mobile experience. Those providers may process information according to their own privacy policies.

Advertising and tracking

The free mobile version may display advertising. Where required, the app requests applicable privacy consent and Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission before requesting ads. Premium subscribers receive an ad-free experience.

Purchases

Premium subscriptions are processed through the applicable app store and managed through RevenueCat. Beautiful Game IQ does not receive your full payment-card information from Apple.

Data retention and deletion

We retain account and team information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service or as required for legitimate business and legal purposes. To request deletion of your account or information, contact support@beautifulgameiq.com.

Children

Beautiful Game IQ is designed as a coaching and team-management tool. Player information entered by coaches should be limited to information reasonably necessary for team administration and coaching. The app is not intended for children to create independent accounts.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact support@beautifulgameiq.com.

Changes

We may update this policy as the service changes. The date above will be updated when material changes are made.
