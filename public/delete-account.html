Beautiful Game IQ Account & Data Deletion

Use this page to request deletion of your Beautiful Game IQ account and the personal data associated with it.

How to request deletion

  1. If you can sign in, you may use Delete Account inside the Beautiful Game IQ app.
  2. Alternatively, send an email to support@beautifulgameiq.com with the subject Beautiful Game IQ account deletion request.
  3. Include the email address associated with your account. Do not send your password.

Request account deletion by email

What is deleted

After we verify and process a request, we delete or arrange deletion of the account and associated personal information held for operating the service, including account profile information and team, roster, lineup, and game data associated with that account, except where retention is necessary for security, dispute resolution, legal compliance, or other lawful purposes.

Retention

We may retain limited records when required by law or reasonably necessary to prevent fraud, enforce our agreements, resolve disputes, or maintain security. Any retained information is kept only for the applicable purpose and period, then deleted or de-identified.

Need help?

Contact support@beautifulgameiq.com if you cannot access your account or have questions about a deletion request.
