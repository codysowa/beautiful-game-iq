Beautiful Game IQ Support

Need help with your team, lineup, game tracking, account, or subscription?

Report a bug

Use Report a Bug inside Beautiful Game IQ whenever possible. Reports include useful diagnostic information so we can investigate the issue.

Delete your account and data

In the app: Open Beautiful Game IQ, go to your account settings, and use Delete Account.

Can't access the app? Request account deletion by emailing support@beautifulgameiq.com with the subject Beautiful Game IQ Account Deletion. Include the email address associated with your account so we can verify the request. Do not include your password.

We will delete your account and associated personal data, including profile and team-related data, after verifying the request, except for information we must retain for legal, security, or dispute-resolution purposes.
